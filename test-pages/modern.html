Your application is reviewed by a loan officer within two working days, and you will get a message as soon as a decision is made about it.

Loan terms depend on your income, your existing obligations, and the collateral you offer. A longer term lowers the monthly payment but raises the total interest you pay over the life of the loan. You can repay early at any time without a fee, and every early payment reduces the interest charged from that day on. If your circumstances change, talk to your branch before a payment is missed, because a restructured plan costs far less than a late one.
